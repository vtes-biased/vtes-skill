## Question

Does the extra cost from Slow Withering come into effect when using the Superior discipline
effect of a card, or is the cost of the card in effect regardless of level chosen as long as it
has a printed Superior effect?

## Answer given

**The +1 blood applies only at the level actually played, not because a superior effect is
printed.** The card taxes *"cards requiring 1 or more Disciplines at the superior level"* — and
what a card requires depends on the version you play: with the superior discipline you *opt*
for either effect (rulebook), and playing the basic effect requires only the basic discipline.
So a dual-level card played at [dom] pays nothing extra; the same card at [DOM] pays +1; a card
whose only mode requires a superior discipline always pays. Timing: the additional cost is paid
together with the normal cost [ANK 20190625] — and if that cost is never reached (the action is
blocked, the card canceled), nothing is paid at all (advanced-rules §1.7).
