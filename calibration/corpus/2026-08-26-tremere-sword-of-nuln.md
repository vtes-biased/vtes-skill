## Question

Has anyone ever tried running a Tremere combat package with a Sword of Nuln?
Like not competetively ofcourse, but maybe a workable concept for causual play. I was thinking
about it with Eulogio for the path of Death.
Since you could only really just block to get into combat effeciently or use the few guys with
celerity to get Hunter's mark.
Now with Path of death I think there could be some uses of oblivion cards for rushing and
maneuvering and maybe use Morbidity since I feel sad about the card not seeing much use.
Any thoughts on the concept?

## Answer given

Workable casual concept, and better-knit than your framing suggests. Three premise checks
first:

- **You're less block-dependent than you think.** Umbrous Clutch is an Oblivion rush: +1
  stealth action, (D) enter combat, superior adds a maneuver. Eulogio (OBL), Anxo Vilela and
  Frau Schädel (both G6, obl/OBL with THA) give you a G5-6 rush core — blocking becomes the
  bonus, not the engine.
- **Morbidity doesn't need Oblivion at all** — it's [obl] *or* [tha], so every Thaumaturgy
  vampire in the deck plays it. You don't need Eulogio to justify it.
- **Hunter's Mark needs cel AND tha**, and the group-legal crew around Eulogio (G4-G7) exists:
  Mason Ha (G6, cap 5), Bituin (G6), Claudio Severino (G5), Troius (G4). Superior is a lovely
  Sword line: the opponent cannot strike and combat ends in round one — one unanswered strength
  hit, out clean.

The real spine is **blood starvation, and Sword of Nuln is its capstone**: Morbidity escrows up
to 2 of their blood for the whole combat (superior also taxes their combat cards +1), Theft of
Vitae strikes take more, and whatever damage lands costs *double* to heal — often against a
vampire whose blood is sitting on your Morbidity. Taste of Vitae explicitly feeds off the
doubled loss by ruling. Add Shadow Shift (maneuver at superior, extra strike at inferior) and
Walk of Flame as the ranged threat and the package has internal synergy, not just a card list.

On **Path of Death and the Soul**, be honest with yourself: it triggers only when another
Methuselah's minion is *burned* — torpor doesn't fire it. It's live in ally-heavy metas or with
a torpor-diablerie plan; otherwise it's a slow fetch. What you actually want from it — Oblivion
master discipline cards on the cel/tha crew — you can just run directly. And the known
weakness, named: this whole plan folds to Majesty-class S:CE walls; superior Hunter's Mark and
repeated cheap Clutch rushes are your grind through it, so don't skimp on the rush count (5-6
Clutch). Sword stays 1× — it's unique, and by ruling it can be passed to a non-Tremere later
via the rulebook equip action, which is a fun casual wrinkle.

## Correction

Five distinct misses:

1. **The build-around premise went unaddressed.** Sword of Nuln is unique — a deck cannot be
   based on it without a fetch engine (Magic of the Smith density), and MotS density in turn
   implies an equipment toolbox (a standard Tremere module). The structural requirement was the
   first thing to say.
2. **Strike-slot competition missed.** Theft of Vitae is a strike and cannot be combined with
   the Sword's strike — the "blood starvation spine" presented them as if they stacked; they
   compete for the one strike per round.
3. **Grouping rule wrong.** A crypt is one group or two *consecutive* groups. Eulogio is G6:
   the choice is G5-G6 or G6-G7. Troius (G4) — offered as group-legal — cannot be paired with
   him; "G4-G7" is not a thing.
4. **Name collision misread.** The player's "Path of Death" is the Sabbat Path that Eulogio,
   Frau Schädel and peers follow (new Sabbat crypt trait) — not the master card "Path of Death
   and the Soul", which shares only the name and a lore reference. The premise-check went to
   the wrong referent.
5. **No archetype baseline, no standard-answers test.** The verdict endorsed the concept
   without comparing it to the classic Carna Toolbox (the combat is not much better than Theft
   of Vitae / Apportation, and other groupings offer better crypts), and without the combat
   survival checks: rush-oriented builds need Telepathic Tracking against S:CE and an answer to
   Immortal Grapple, or the deck does not survive most pot-based combat.
