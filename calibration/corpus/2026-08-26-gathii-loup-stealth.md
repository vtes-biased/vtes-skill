## Question

If Gathi uses his ability when he does not need stealth, he will not gain any even if he flips
non-master card.
Should this also mean that while I can play Loup when stealth is not needed, it will not
actually give +1 stealth to Toreador?

## Answer given

**No — the Gathii logic does not transfer, because the two effects have different shapes.** The
general rule: an effect that *adds* stealth cannot be used when stealth isn't needed
(advanced-rules §3.2.1). Gathii's ability got an exception because it's a gamble (it may burn
blood instead), so it *can* be used early — but its stealth is a **one-shot gain evaluated at
that moment**, and a gain you don't need evaporates [ANK 20231031]. Loup doesn't grant a
one-shot gain at all: it **puts a card in play** whose text is a continuous *"Toreador get +1
stealth"*. Its ruling allows playing it when stealth isn't needed [LSJ 20011214-2], and the +1
then simply sits on every Toreador for as long as Loup remains in play — it counts whenever
stealth is actually compared against intercept, including later in the same action if a
blocker wakes. Continuous modifiers aren't "gained" at a moment; they're evaluated whenever
needed.
