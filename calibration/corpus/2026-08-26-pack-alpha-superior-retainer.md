## Question

If you use Pack Alpha to recruit employ a retainer, is it allowed for the retainer to come out
with the superior discipline or is it required to be inferior?

## Answer given

**Superior is allowed — and never required.** Pack Alpha [ani] says *"Employ an animal retainer
from your hand (requirements and cost apply as normal)"*, and the normal rule is that a vampire
with the superior discipline *"may opt to use either the basic (plain text) or the superior
(bold) effect listed on the card"* (rulebook, Disciplines). So a vampire with superior
Animalism employing, say, Murder of Crows via Pack Alpha chooses either version — [ANI] with 2
life, or [ani] with 1. With only basic Animalism, basic is the only option. One side note from
the card's rulings: this employ is not an action, so cost reductions worded around an action
(Charisma) don't apply, while plain card-cost reductions do [ANK 20230824].
