## Question

Anyone had any luck with Business Pressure and Awe in Toreador vote decks? In my current meta I
often see barons/princes even without much focus on politics, but when they get a Sabbat vote
as a cross-table / or just another vote-loaded deck, my game is set back by a mile, having zero
change to advance my political actions, unless I get lucky redirects/my predator is afk. So I'm
thinking about ramping up my vote strength. Currently I run Paragons and Bewitching Oration.
Already have few Conclaves and Versailles to help a bit.

## Answer given

The two cards are not peers. **Awe is the staple you're missing**: 50 tournament-winning decks
since 2022 carry it, 74% of them next to your Perfect Paragons, 100% next to Voter Captivation.
At superior it's +2X+1 votes — 2 blood for +5 — and X=0 still gives +1, so it never jams. The
blood spend is exactly what Voter Captivation refills, which is why the pairing is universal.
I'd add 3-4× before anything else.

**Business Pressure is fringe for a reason** — 4 winning decks since 2022, all one Toreador
Grand Ball list family. Read it carefully: it's *symmetric*. Every Methuselah may burn pool for
votes; your edge is only +3 per pool against their +2. Against exactly the opponent that's
hurting you — another vote-loaded deck — it hands them a vote engine too, and turns referendums
into pool wars you may not win while paying pool you defend with. It's a finisher for when
you're pool-ahead and must force one referendum through, not a vote-strength plan. 1× as a
lunge tool at most.

Your structural answers against baron/Sabbat tables are already half in your deck:

- **Camarilla Conclave's second mode is the tech**: -1 vote to *each non-Camarilla vampire* per
  counter — barons and Sabbat titles are all non-Cam, so one or two counters can erase a
  cardinal outright. (Mind the requirement: you need the justicar or Inner Circle member on the
  table to play it.)
- **Versailles scales with your crypt, not your hand** — its ceiling is how many titled
  Camarilla vampires you deploy. Inherent votes are the strength that doesn't cost cards or
  blood: weight the crypt toward princes/justicars before adding more polling cards.
- **Table play**: a cross-table vote deck is a customer, not just an obstacle — the point of
  politics is damage-distribution freedom. Splitting KRC points their way on the referendums
  you both want buys their votes for the ones you need; out-voting them every poll is the
  expensive way.
