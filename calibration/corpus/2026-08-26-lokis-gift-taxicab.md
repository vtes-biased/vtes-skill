## Question

A Vampire with an Unlicensed Taxicap performs an Lokis Gift on Superior. Is there the extra
Stealth from the Taxi? I would say yes, because its still an hunt action. But on superior it
gets an (D) Action, and that makes ist a little bit unsure for me.

## Answer given

**Yes — the Taxicab's +1 stealth applies, at both levels of Loki's Gift.** Your instinct is
right, and the Ⓓ is a red herring.

- **It stays a hunt action.** Advanced-rules §3.7.2.2, with rulings behind it: *"A hunt action
  granted by a card is a hunt action for every purpose."* Loki's Gift's type line is "+1
  stealth hunt action", and the superior text is *"Ⓓ As above…"* — the same hunt, with extras
  bolted on.
- **The Ⓓ changes who, not what.** The directed marker at superior exists because the action
  now targets another vampire ("burn 1 blood from any vampire") — it affects who the action is
  directed at and thus who may block it. It does not change the action's type. Type and
  directedness are independent properties.
- Unlicensed Taxicab reads *"This minion gets +1 stealth during hunt, recruit and employ
  actions"* — a superior Loki's Gift is a hunt action, so the condition is met.

Net: the action runs at **2 stealth** (the card's printed +1 stealth + the Taxicab's +1),
before anything else. One adjacent trap worth knowing: effects worded against *the normal
hunt* (e.g. Igo the Hungry stripping "the normal +1 stealth when hunting") do **not** touch a
card-provided hunt like Loki's Gift — that stealth is printed on the card, not the rulebook
hunt bonus (§3.7.2.2, [ANK/LSJ per §3.7.2.4 note]).
