# Community sources — annotated locator index

Curated blogs from the wider VTES community, embarked as a **lower knowledge tier** below the
digests in the authority hierarchy:

> **Community commentary — never authority for rules.** A community post citing a ruling
> (RTR id, forum link) is a *locator*: verify against the rulebook / rulings canon / API
> before relying on it. For strategy, community takes are a secondary voice below the carried
> codex material: cite with author and date, never present as the skill's own calibrated view.

None of these sources grants a reuse license (the one exception, KGS Cards, gave the owner
permission — its judge-grade corpus is carried in `kgs-cards.md`). Everything else is
consulted **over the network at need**: fetch the post, use it, cite it. WordPress.com sites
return 403 to naive fetchers — use a browser User-Agent (`curl -A "Mozilla/5.0 ..."`).
Blogger sites expose a JSON feed (`<site>/feeds/posts/default?alt=json`) that lists and
serves full posts. Archive.org capture URLs are recorded per source as rot insurance.

Statuses and dates below as of the evaluation pass, 2026-08-26 — update on touch.

## KGS Cards — https://blog.kgs-cards.com

Dormant (last post 2025-06); 40 posts, 2020-2025; pseudonymous author, ex-BCP-playtest.
The expert corpus — the "Judge's Notebook" rules post and the six-part Crypt Construction
math series — is **carried verbatim with the author's permission** in
`references/community/kgs-cards.md`; read that file directly, no fetch needed.

Fetch the live blog for the rest: V5-only format previews, "How Variants Improve Skill"
deckbuilding series (2023), New Blood / Fall of London set reviews, design commentary
("Contesting Changes That Didn't Happen", 2025 — informed by a BCP closed playtest).
Archive: http://web.archive.org/web/20260521161016/https://blog.kgs-cards.com/

## Information Highway — https://vtesinformationhighway.wordpress.com

Alive (2026-08); 105 posts since 2018; authored by credentialed competitive players —
Serge "Orpheus" (French Champion 2006, VTES Hall of Famer) and contributors.

Consult for: **current-year meta claims** — the annual "Best clans of 20XX" TWDA analyses
state their methodology (VDB crypt-composition thresholds for clan/sect attribution) and are
the best community complement to our own `rates`/TWDA queries; deep archetype theory (e.g.
"The art of Combat" series); "New Blood" clan guides and card guides. No judge-level rules
content. Archive: http://web.archive.org/web/20260129082931/https://vtesinformationhighway.wordpress.com/

## VTESOne — https://vtesone.wordpress.com

Alive (2026-08); 2,615 posts since 2007 — the largest community archive; author "extrala".

Consult for: the **"Did you know" rules-interaction series** (147 parts and running,
judge-precise, cites ruling ids — locators to verify against canon); the 2010-2015
**Deck Archetypes** essays (long-form expert write-ups of classic archetypes with game plans
and matchups); VTES history timeline, expansion and format/variant reference pages. Skip:
the current news/results stream — it reposts VEKN.net.
Archive: http://web.archive.org/web/20260421200344/https://vtesone.wordpress.com/

## The Thessalian Scrolls — https://thethessalianscrolls.blogspot.com

Revived after a 2.5-year gap (2023 posts + one 2026-08 post); 14 posts; author "Pritoos".

Consult for: **quarterly/half-year metagame statistics** — ~4,500-word aggregate analyses of
worldwide tournament results (clan/archetype performance, card frequencies, capacity trends,
participation by country). Raw data duplicates TWDA/Archon we already ground on; the value is
the trend analysis. Coverage hole: 2024-2025.
Archive: http://web.archive.org/web/20260416151851/https://thethessalianscrolls.blogspot.com/

## Damian's Quest — https://vtesdamiansquest.wordpress.com

Alive (2026-06); 21 posts since 2025-06; author is Prince of Bedford (UK), EC-day-2-level
competitor, plays 20-30 concurrent JOL games.

Consult for: **play-level decision essays** (risk/reward evaluation, overcommitment, table
pressure-chain models — e.g. "Risks, Rewards and Santa Claus", "Just because you can, it
doesn't mean you should") and the only expert coverage anywhere of **JOL / asynchronous
online play**; current UK-scene tournament reports with real deck-selection reasoning.

## Bindusara's Jyhad Diary — https://bindusara.home.blog

Concluded (2019-01 to 2020-01, deliberately closed); 87 posts. **History, not operations** —
near-zero rules or strategy content.

Consult for: **designer intent and oral history** — long-form interviews with Richard
Garfield & Skaff Elias (original design), L. Scott Johnson (rules stewardship), Andrew
Greenberg, Justin Achilli, artists (Ken Meyer Jr, Brian Le Blanc…), and prominent players
(Ben Peal, Robert Goudie…). Cite as historical color only.
Archive: http://web.archive.org/web/20260104171334/https://bindusara.home.blog/
