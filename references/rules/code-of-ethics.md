# VEKN Code of Ethics
v1.5 — Effective 9 September 2019

## Key Entities
- **VEKN**: Official Players' Organization for VTES. Sanctions/regulates tournaments, maintains ratings/rankings.
- **Princes**: Local volunteers coordinating tournaments/demos.
- **National Coordinators**: Regional oversight, approve tournament sanctioning.
- **Black Chantry Productions (BCP)**: VTES publisher. Internalized governance from VEKN (Aug 2018). Responsibilities: card revision/development/publishing, rulings/errata/tournament rules, player rating system, official communications.
- **Ethics Committee**: Chaired by BCP's Organized Play Coordinator. 3 VEKN members + 1 alternate. The relevant National Coordinator joins for investigations (total 5 reviewers).

## Scope
Applies to all participants in VEKN activities equally, regardless of role/title. Concerns VEKN membership rights/privileges only (tournament participation, player rating, VEKN infrastructure access). No relation to criminal law.

## Standards of Conduct
All conduct must be: lawful locally, per VEKN rules, consistent with fair play/honesty/respect.

### Unethical conduct (non-exhaustive):
- Intentionally violating game/tournament rules (especially after prior warning)
- Cheating by altering deck during event
- Deliberately misrepresenting rules or game state
- Colluding to predetermine game winner
- Failing to play to win per Tournament Rules 4.8, especially to determine winner
- Participating under false name or while suspended
- Significant impairment from alcohol/drugs at event
- Barring active unsuspended player from sanctioned event
- Submitting falsified tournament report
- Slurs/derogatory language targeting race, ethnicity, sex, gender, sexual orientation, disability, class, religion, age, or other identity
- Defaming VEKN members (slander/libel)
- Continued unwanted social advances after rebuff
- Threatening/intimidating/assaulting anyone in venue
- Violating laws during VEKN activities

## Procedures

**Contact**: orgplay@blackchantry (electronic submissions; translation available)

**Confidentiality**: Committee protects abuse victims' identity. May publicly name subjects of harassment complaints for safety. Will not name victims without consent.

### On-site Sanctions
- Reserved for clear, gross violations at events meriting more than disqualification
- Only head judge may issue
- Player immediately DQ'd and suspended from VEKN events
- Duration: 30 days, applies only to nation where issued
- Must be noted in tournament report
- Head judge must initiate Ethics Committee review within 5 calendar days or suspension lifts

### Independently Initiated Sanctions
Ethics Committee may independently review behavior inside or outside events (exceedingly rare, for egregious violations).

### Review (all sanctions)
- Committee reviews within 14 days of receipt
- Lifted if review not completed in 14 days
- Requires 3/5 majority to uphold
- If not upheld, player returned to active status
- Accused player is interviewed; may provide witness list
- Committee member under review is replaced by alternate

### Appeals
No appeal process. Ethics Committee determination is final.

## Sanction Levels
All sanctions are worldwide.

- **Standard suspension**: Prohibited from VEKN events. Duration: remainder of 30-day term up to 18 months from incident date. May lose all VEKN privileges including website access.
- **Probation**: Retains all VEKN privileges. Max 12 months. Further violations trigger mandatory suspension (remaining probation added to suspension term). One probation per VEKN career.
- **Ban**: Permanent. Removed from ratings. May lose all VEKN access.

### Additional rules
- Suspended members forfeit organizational roles (Prince, NC, etc.) for 2x suspension duration (unless Ethics Committee unanimously decides otherwise)
- TOs/judges must allow all unsuspended registered players into sanctioned events. Failure may result in Prince status forfeiture, tournament invalidation, and sanctions.
- Probationary/suspended/banned player list published at vekn.net
