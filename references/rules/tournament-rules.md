# VEKN Tournament Rules

## 1. General Rules

### 1.1 Player Eligibility
All players eligible for open VEKN-sanctioned tournaments except:
- Tournament organizer (unless Multi-Judge System, see 2.9)
- Head judge and listed judges (exception: Multi-Judge System)
- VEKN-suspended players
- Players prohibited by VEKN/Black Chantry policy

### 1.2 Required Materials
- Counters, pen/paper for game info tracking
- Valid unique VEKN number (new players register at first tournament; one number per player)
- Format-specific materials (e.g., assembled deck for Constructed)

### 1.3 Wagering
No wagering/betting on tournament outcomes.

### 1.4 Notes
Players may take written notes if it doesn't slow play.

### 1.5 Electronic Devices
Head judge/organizer may prohibit electronic devices.

### 1.6 Publishing
Black Chantry and organizer may publish event info including decklists and recordings.

### 1.7 Updates
VEKN may alter rules without prior notice.

## 2. Responsibilities

### 2.1 Event Knowledge
All participants must know current Tournament Rules, game rules, and errata. New cards/rules effective on release date. Errata effective as stated (e.g., reprinted card text valid from new set's release date).

### 2.2 Organizer
Responsible for: site selection, materials, retaining results 1 year, reporting to VEKN, staffing (including head judge), advertising. May delegate.

### 2.3 Players
Must follow rules and judge rulings. Sportsmanlike conduct required. Cannot waive penalties for opponents.

### 2.4 Spectators
Anyone not playing or judging. Ousted players become spectators. Must remain silent, no communication with players. Report rule violations to judge only. Players may request spectator removal through judge.

### 2.5 Judges
Deliver fair rulings, assist head judge, act on all rule infractions noticed or reported.

### 2.6 Head Judge
Must be physically present. May appoint additional judges. Final judicial authority. Reports all warnings. May also be organizer.

### 2.7 Appeals
Players may appeal judge decisions to head judge. Must wait for initial ruling first. Head judge decision is final.

### 2.8 Lengthy Rulings
If ruling takes >1 minute, judge may extend game time (must communicate and record immediately).

### 2.9 Multi-Judge System
Available at non-qualifier, non-championship events with 8+ participants. Must announce before tournament. Designate 6 judges (head, secondary, 3rd-6th). Allows organizer and judges to participate as players. Rulings cascade: head judge rules unless seated in the game, then secondary, etc.

## 3. Tournament Mechanics

### 3.1 Round Structure
Minimum 3 rounds. After 2+ preliminary rounds, rank by: 1) Game Wins 2) total VPs 3) Tournament Points. Remaining ties for top 5 broken randomly. Top 5 advance to final.

#### 3.1.1 Time Limits
Minimum 2 hours per round. Must announce before play. Finals may be longer. When time is called, game ends after current minion action (if during minion phase) or at end of current phase.

#### 3.1.2 Preliminary Seating
Random impartial assignment. Groups of 5 preferred; groups of 4 and 5 if not divisible by 5. For 6, 7, or 11 players, judge may use sit-out arrangement. 5 positions clockwise; position 1 plays first. Judge must prevent duplicate predator-prey relationships between rounds when possible, reseating impartially if needed.

#### 3.1.3 Final Seating
No assigned positions. Each finalist shuffles crypt, presents to judge. Judge draws 3 random crypt cards from each (revealed publicly with crypt size). Starting from lowest qualifier, each places name card at either end of row or between two existing cards. Left-to-right = seating order. Judge randomly determines first player. Revealed crypt cards shuffled back.

#### 3.1.4 Bowing Out
Must inform scorekeeper before next round seatings generated. If after seatings begin: receive Loss for upcoming round, then removed.

#### 3.1.5 Multi-Deck System
Cannot be used at national/Grand Prix/continental championships. Must announce in advance. Players may bring multiple legal decks and/or sideboard cards. May switch decks/cards between rounds.

**Multi-Deck Finals:** Organizer must announce finals deck method before round 1:
- **Best-Performing Deck:** Must play deck with best single-round performance (GW > VP > TP). Ties broken randomly.
- **Free Choice:** May play any deck used in prelims.

#### 3.1.6 Tournament Without Final
For <8 players: organizer may omit final or run additional prelim round instead (announce before round 1). Prelim results determine standings. Cannot admit players between rounds if it would create 8+ players. Compatible with multi-deck system.

### 3.2 Pre-Game Procedure
Order: 1) Shuffle decks 2) Present to predator for optional shuffle/cut 3) Draw 7 library + 4 crypt cards. All shuffling face-hidden. Decks must be sufficiently randomized. After predator shuffle, owner may make one final cut. Disputes go to head judge.

### 3.3 Tardiness
Players must be seated when round begins. >15 min late = Loss for round.

#### 3.3.1 Late Arrivals
May be added at judge's discretion, starting next round. Cannot join games in progress.

#### 3.3.2 Early Departure
Notify judge ASAP. If seatings already set, judge may treat as Bowing Out (3.1.4).

#### 3.3.3 In-game Breaks
Must notify judge before taking break. Time extended as needed.

### 3.4 Pre-Game Shuffling
Competitors shuffle and present decks to opponents. Shuffling as stalling = Judge's Guide penalty.

### 3.5 Conceding
All but one player must agree to concede. Must not violate play-to-win. Remaining player recorded as having ousted conceding players in sequence. Bribery/coercion to concede = penalty.

### 3.6 Intentional Draw
Mutual agreement required before results submitted. If declined, no further coercion. Same result as time limit.

### 3.7 Game Winner / Scoring

Three scoring types:
- **Game Win (GW):** Awarded if player has 2+ VP and more VP than any other player (no GW on ties)
- **Victory Points (VP):** 1 VP per prey ousted; 0.5 VP for withdrawal (not full VP); 0.5 VP for surviving at time limit (unless last player standing = 1 VP)
- **Tournament Points (TP):** Based on table ranking

#### 3.7.3 Tournament Points

| Place | 5-player table | 4-player table |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | 60 | 60 |
| 2nd | 48 | 48* |
| 3rd | 36 | (empty/bye) |
| 4th | 24 | 24* |
| 5th | 12 | 12 |

*4-player: ranks are 1st, 2nd, 4th, 5th (3rd = table bye). Tied ranks: average their TP.

**Example (5p):** A,C each oust 1 prey + survive = 1.5 VP each. E survives = 0.5 VP. B,D ousted = 0 VP. A,C tie 1st/2nd = 54 TP each ((60+48)/2). No GW (tied, <2 VP). E = 3rd = 36 TP. B,D tie 4th/5th = 18 TP each.

**Example (4p):** A ousts 1 + survives = 1.5 VP. C,D survive = 0.5 VP each. B ousted = 0. A = 1st = 60 TP. C,D tie 2nd/4th = 36 TP each ((48+24)/2). B = 5th = 12 TP.

#### 3.7.5 Final Round Scoring
Winner = highest VP in final round only. Tie broken by prelim rankings. Other finalists tie for 2nd (no further tiebreaking).

## 4. Game Rules

### 4.1 Cards Allowed
Genuine VTES/Jyhad cards or official VEKN cards from vekn.net:
- Danse Macabre, The Unaligned, Storyline Rewards 2015, Anarchs Unbound

VEKN cards: preferably color-printed on standard paper in opaque sleeves before a real VTES/Jyhad card. Not considered proxies. Mixed card backs require sufficiently mixed types or opaque sleeves.

#### 4.1.1 Banned List
- Anthelios, the Red Star
- Dramatic Upheaval
- Edge Explosion
- Gypsies
- Kindred Restructure
- Lilith's Blessing
- Madness of the Bard
- Memories of Mortality
- Protect Thine Own
- The Return to Innocence
- Rom Gypsy
- Seeds of Corruption
- Succubus Club
- Tarbaby Jack
- Temptation of Greater Power
- Terrorists
- Tsigane
- Ante-only cards: Cunctator Motion, High Stakes, Playing for Keeps

Nights of Reckoning rules cards are legal in both Constructed and Limited.

### 4.2 Card Interpretation
Head judge is final authority. Use most recent printing text. Misleading older text = Judge's Guide penalty.

### 4.3 New Releases
Constructed: legal 30 days after retail release (VEKN confirms exact date). Limited: legal immediately, including prereleases.

### 4.4 Card Elevation
Cards must be kept above table surface. No revealing hand/uncontrolled region cards to opponents.

### 4.5 Proxy Cards
Organizer may allow proxies at events that are not National Championship, Grand Prix, or Continental Championship:
- No limit on proxy count
- Must be color copy of original on standard paper
- Illustrator name must be readable
- Event marked as proxy-allowed on VEKN calendar
- In opaque sleeve before a real VTES/Jyhad card
- No custom artwork/text (except game tokens like The Embrace)

Judge-issued proxies: for cards damaged during tournament or when owner must leave. Original kept nearby; swap proxy/original when card enters/leaves play.

### 4.6 Card Sleeves
Optional but if used, all cards in deck sleeved identically. Holograms/markings must be uniform. Crypt and library may be sleeved differently. Judge may inspect and disallow marked/worn sleeves.

### 4.7 Locked Cards
Turn ~90 degrees from normal position.

### 4.8 Play to Win
Must play toward game goals: GW if reasonably possible, otherwise maximize VP. Deals allowed if in best interest of involved players when made (may also be broken). During finals: play to be tournament winner. Exception: when only 2 Methuselahs remain, all prior deals voided; must play to win based on game state only.

### 4.9 Infinite Loops
When game state completely restored to previous state (including hands, ash heaps, libraries, available maneuvers/presses, master out-of-turn plays), the loop activity cannot be repeated until game state changes.

## 5. Violations

### 5.1 Cheating
Includes: outside assistance, looking at card faces during shuffle/cut, collusion, misrepresenting rules/cards/errata, marked cards, drawing extra cards, deck manipulation, stalling, misrepresenting/obscuring public info. All disqualifications subject to VEKN review.

### 5.2 Unsportsmanlike Conduct
No profanity, arguing, belligerence, harassment. Applies to judges, players, spectators, officials.

### 5.3 Slow Play
Must play timely. Reasonable thinking time acceptable; stalling is not.

### 5.4 Marked Cards
Card is marked if identifiable without seeing face (scratches, discoloration, bends). Clear sleeves: examine card+sleeve. Opaque sleeves: examine sleeve only.

## 6. Constructed Rules

### 6.1 Formats
One sanctioned format: Standard Constructed.

#### 6.1.1 Set Restrictions
Organizer may limit card sets (must advertise). Use most recent card text regardless. Events with set restrictions excluded from VEKN ratings.

### 6.2 Deck Size
Library: 60-90 cards. Crypt: minimum 12.

### 6.3 Deck Registration
Judge/organizer may require decklist registration. Once submitted, deck cannot be altered. Organizers should check decks against lists each round.

### 6.4 V5 Format
Standard Constructed with reduced card pool for current World of Darkness setting.

Card pool:
- 14 Fifth Edition precons: Banu Haqim, Brujah, Gangrel, Hecata, Lasombra, Malkavian, Ministry, Nosferatu, Ravnos, Salubri, Toreador, Tremere, Tzimisce, Ventrue
- 11 New Blood packs: Banu, Brujah, Gangrel, Hecata, Lasombra, Malkavian, Ministry, Nosferatu, Toreador, Tremere, Ventrue
- Fall of London, Shadows of Berlin, 30th Anniversary, Promo Pack 3 (Icons), Promo Pack 4 (Rebels)
- Sabbat V5 precons (2025-10-26): Path of Cain, Path of Cathari, Path of the Dead and the Soul, Path of the Power and the Inner Voice
- New Blood (TBD): Ravnos, Salubri, Tzimisce

New releases added on release date. Any legal print version of pool cards is valid.

## 7. Limited Rules

### 7.1 Formats
Sealed: Standard Sealed (7.3), Restricted Trade (7.4)
Draft: Booster Draft (7.6), Table/Rochester Draft (7.7), Mixed/Elder Draft (7.8)
Alternative Constructed: Restricted format (7.9)

### 7.2 General Limited Rules

#### 7.2.1 Deck Composition
If starters used: library min 40, crypt min 12.
Boosters only (assuming 11-card boosters; for other sizes, divide total cards by 11, round):

| Boosters | Min Crypt | Min Library | Recursions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12+ | 12 | 40 | 0 |
| 11 | 11 | 40 | 0 |
| 10 | 10 | 40 | 0 |
| 9 | 9 | 40 | 0 |
| 8 | 8 | 40 | 0 |
| 7 | 7 | 35 | 1 |
| 6 | 6 | 30 | 1 |
| 5 | 5 | 25 | 1 |
| 4 | 4 | 20 | 2 |
| 3 | 3 | 15 | 3 |
| 2 | 2 | 10 | 5 |
| 1 | 1 | 5 | 11 |

Library max always 90. Crypts may mix any groups.

#### 7.2.2 Recursion
When library min <40: discard phase action to shuffle all library cards from ash heap into library. Player not considered to have exhausted library if recursions remain. Effective library size targets ~60.

#### 7.2.3 Deck Registration
Judge/organizer may require recording all received cards. Limited construction time.

#### 7.2.4 Card Use
All cards from tournament officials. Identical sealed product per player (starters may vary, distributed randomly; organizer may allow starter trading before opening). Alternatively, players may bring own sealed product with organizer-specified restrictions. No trading/replacing drafted/received cards with outside cards.

#### 7.2.5 Abnormal Cards/Boosters
Report to head judge, who may replace. No guaranteed rarity distribution.

#### 7.2.6 Early Departure / Late Arrivals
Cannot leave before round 1 concludes (must return product, receive Loss, dropped). Late arrivals: may be added with sealed deck rules (not draft), may join after deck construction.

### 7.3 Standard Sealed
Players receive sealed product, build deck within time limit. Judge may require decklist.

#### 7.3.2 Sealed Deck Swap
Optional. Players open and register received product. 10-20% returned to registerers; rest redistributed randomly. Players build from final product.

### 7.4 Restricted Trade (Michigan Draft)
Standard Sealed rules plus:
- Library: build exactly 50 cards + select 15 unused library cards as trade stock
- Crypt: build 10 cards + keep remaining as sideboard
- Assemble into 4-5 player pods
- Trading: active player (rotating left after each accepted trade) selects faceup card from another player
  - Accept: owner selects active player's faceup card; both go facedown
  - Decline: selected card goes facedown; active player can't select from that player again this turn
- Trading ends when all cards facedown
- Post-trade: add 2 crypt cards from sideboard (=12 total), shuffle 15 trade stock into library (=65 total)

### 7.5 General Draft Rules
Random pods of roughly equal size. Cards placed in two piles (library/crypt). Drafted cards reviewed only between packs.

### 7.6 Booster Draft
Open pack, take 1 card, pass remaining facedown left. Alternate direction each pack (1st left, 2nd right, etc.). Report abnormal pack count to judge immediately.

### 7.7 Table Draft (Rochester Draft)
Cards laid faceup. Must display most recent drafted card faceup.

**Preparation:** Open pack facedown, count, move crypt to top. Place facedown. At signal, place top 2 cards faceup. 20-second review.

**Rotation:** Active player rotates left after each set. Horseshoe pattern: active player picks 1st, clockwise to last player who picks 2, then reverse back. Each player gets 2 cards per set.

**Example (5 players, active=P1):** P1-P2-P3-P4-P5-P5-P4-P3-P2-P1. P2 becomes active for next set.

Late draft = judge assigns oldest remaining card.

### 7.8 Mixed Draft (Elder Draft)
Open all packs, count facedown, separate crypt from library. Draft crypt via Table Draft (7.7). Then split library into organizer-specified pile sizes, draft via Booster Draft (7.6).

### 7.9 Restricted Format
Organizer sets deck content restrictions (e.g., Sabbat-only crypt, Black Chantry cards only, Jyhad-backed only). Must indicate in tournament calendar.

## 8. Sanctioning Rules
Apply for sanctioning 28+ days before event. Advertise 28+ days before.

## 9. Reporting Rules

### 9.1 Records
Organizers keep tournament reports 1 year.

### 9.2 Deadline
Reports due within 8 days. Listed as "Not Received" for 14 days after event if late.

### 9.3 Delinquent
15-30 days late = "Delinquent" (can still submit without penalty).

### 9.4 Invalid
VEKN may invalidate results (usually for fraud/incorrect reporting). Reports >30 days late may be invalidated. Invalid event records don't count for ratings.

### 9.5 Status Updates
Check status on VEKN website. 2 consecutive months Delinquent/Invalid = VEKN investigation.

### 9.6 VEKN Numbers
All participants need VEKN number before playing. Temp numbers/names/placeholders not included in ratings.

### 9.7 Reports and Invitations
Reports must meet VEKN deadlines for inclusion in ratings for invitation/bye lists.

### 9.8 Non-Open Events
1) Coordinator outlines qualification process to orgplay@blackchantry for approval
2) Qualifiers run as regular tournaments, open to all
3) Non-open event sanctioned independently through orgplay@blackchantry

## Appendix A - Rating System

Ratings groups: Standard Constructed (on-site), Standard Constructed Online, Limited (on-site), Limited Online.

### A.1 Rating
Single number (RtP). Ranked from first tournament. Removed from rankings after 12 months inactivity (stats kept indefinitely).

### A.2 Computing Rating
Points accumulation over preceding 18 months. Best 8 tournaments count.

Per tournament:
- 5 RtP for attending
- 4 RtP per VP scored
- 8 RtP per GW (including final round victory)
- Finalist bonus (see A.2.1)

Player count includes DQ'd and early-departure players who played 1+ round.

#### A.2.1 Finalist Bonus
| Standing | Base Points |
|---|---|
| Winner | 90 |
| Finalist | 30 |
| Non-finalist | 0 |

Coefficient = log15(NumPlayers^2) - 1

Bonus = Base Points x Coefficient

Coefficient adjustments: National championship +0.25, Continental championship +1.0, Grand Prix/Qualifiers +0.

Example: 50-player qualifier: Coef = log15(2500) - 1 = 2.889 - 1 = 1.889. Winner bonus = 1.889 x 90 = 170 RtP.

## Appendix B - Definitions

- **Ante Card:** Card mentioning "ante" in rules text; found in older expansions
- **Banned Card:** Not allowed in indicated format
- **Constructed:** Players bring own decks
- **Cutting:** Removing single portion of deck, placing on top of remainder, without seeing faces (more = shuffle)
- **Game Begins:** Once all decks presented to predators for shuffle/cut
- **Limited:** Decks built at tournament from opened packs
- **Premier Events:** Events limited to select organizers/players (Worlds, Continental, National, Regional, State Championships, storyline, prereleases)
- **Promo Card:** Playable card released outside any set
- **Proxy Card:** Card representing another card; includes counterfeits
- **Public Information:** Info available to all players (pool totals, library card count, etc.)
- **Rating:** Numeric value indicating past tournament performance
- **Ranking:** Player's position relative to comparison group
- **Round:** Period of gameplay
- **Round Begins:** Posted/announced time for players to be seated
- **Scorekeeper:** Official handling results, seatings, player management
- **Tournament Begins:** When onsite registration closes
- **Tournament Official:** Any empowered tournament staff (organizer, judges, scorekeeper)
- **VEKN:** Vampire Elder Kindred Network, VTES Players Organization
- **VTES:** Vampire: The Eternal Struggle
